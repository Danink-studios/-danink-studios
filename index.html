About the artist

Art built for real spaces.

Danink Studios is the creative practice of Danson Maina, a Kenya-based visual artist focused on murals, illustration and original painted artwork. The work shown here spans large commercial walls, hospitality spaces, gates, entertainment interiors, wildlife subjects, portraits and decorative collections.

The approach is practical as well as artistic: understand the brief, develop the visual direction, adapt the composition to the site, paint with care, then refine the final presentation.
